## Flagged risks

- **C8-016 (TT 114/2021).** Checked on the web (caselaw.vn, plus thoibaotaichinhvietnam.vn and luatvietnam.vn): TT 114/2021/TT-BTC, dated 17/12/2021, repeals the whole of TT 146/2014 (Điều 1). Điều 2 sets out how old fund balances are handled. The circular took effect on 01/02/2022, applies from financial year 2022, and is still in force. The item stays CM with this exact basis. The stem and options were almost a copy of PL-0632, so the item was rewritten as our own scenario: a CTCK with VĐL of 1.000 tỷ đồng and LNST of 120 tỷ đồng whose ĐHĐCĐ skips both funds. The distractors are the old rules worked out in money (6 tỷ, 12 tỷ, and the 100 tỷ cap).
- **C8-012 (Bộ luật Hình sự).** Checked against VBHN 135/VBHN-VPQH of 05/9/2025, which consolidates Luật 12/2017 and Luật 86/2025 (read on hethongphapluat.com). Điều 76 still lists 209, 210, 211 and 213 among the offences for which a commercial legal entity is liable, and 212 is not on the list. The competitor's DC-423 used the same scenario and the same "yes, if the elements are met" key, so the item was rewritten as a NEGATIVE question: which securities offence an entity is NOT liable for (key: Điều 212, falsifying documents in an offering or listing file). Note: a draft new Criminal Code was published in 08/2026 but has not taken effect.
- **C8-013 (Luật Khiếu nại).** Luật 136/2025/QH15 amended the Law on Complaints with effect from 01/07/2026. In a consolidated text that includes it (published by Lạng Sơn province), Điều 9 has no amendment footnote and the limitation period is still 90 days. The competitor's PL-0670 and DC-506 ask "how many days?", so the item became a date calculation: decision received 14/10/2026, last day 12/01/2027 (a weekday). The distractors are "3 months", 60 days and 30 days. Level changed to 3.
- **C8-010 (Luật Doanh nghiệp).** Checked Điều 155 khoản 2 điểm c on hethongphapluat.com. The list of amended articles of Luật 76/2025/QH15 does not include Điều 155, and VBHN 67/VBHN-VPQH/2025 lists 03/2022 and 76/2025 as the only amending laws. The key depends on LDN, not on the NĐ 155 cross-reference that was quoted, so the item was **moved to CM/CB** ("Quản lý, giám sát thị trường chứng khoán"). Its quote and src were removed, and the basis now names the exact provision.
- **C8-008 (Vietnamese quote).** VBHN_17 in `van_ban_txt` holds only 202 bytes, just the signature block, and `ND_306_2025_ND-CP_signed.txt` holds 80 bytes. Both PDFs are scans and no OCR tool is installed, so there is no Vietnamese text to quote. The item keeps the official English SSC translation (the precedent is L2-091), and the basis now says why. The quote was widened so it covers the officials named. The stem was reversed to ask which official has the 2,4 tỷ / 1,2 tỷ limit, because PL-0711 asked the same question in the same direction. The distractors are checked as follows: the head of an SSC inspectorate team is limited to 1,5 tỷ (new khoản 1a); the Chairman's powers under khoản 2 were not changed by NĐ 306 (NĐ 128 changed only điểm d); the Exchange has no sanctioning power.
