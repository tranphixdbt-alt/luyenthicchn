## Flagged risks
- **Thanh tra chứng khoán vs Luật Thanh tra 2025.** Khoản 2 Điều 62 Luật Thanh tra 2025 says other laws on inspection organisation that do not conform must be amended before 01/3/2027, and that Luật Thanh tra applies until then. Điều 129 Luật CK (unchanged in VBHN 102/2026) still describes "Thanh tra chứng khoán" as a specialised inspectorate under the professional guidance of "Thanh tra Bộ Tài chính". Điều 7 Luật Thanh tra 2025 lists neither. NĐ 306/2025 (25/11/2025) still names "Chánh Thanh tra Chứng khoán Nhà nước" and "Trưởng đoàn thanh tra Thanh tra Chứng khoán Nhà nước". The texts do not settle whether the Điều 129 body still exists, so:
  - **L4-017** rested on Art 129.4(b), which is contested → rewritten around the uncontested conflict rule (Art 62.2 Luật Thanh tra 2025: amend before 01/3/2027, apply Luật Thanh tra meanwhile). The explanation states only what the texts say. a = 0.
  - **L4-029**: kept. Its stem already cites khoản 1a Điều 47 (NĐ 306), and the key (1.5 bn/750 m) is in the quote. The slide note was removed from the explanation.
  - **C8-008** (cm_8.json): kept. Its stem rests on NĐ 156 as amended by NĐ 306, and the quote is cl. 1 Art 47. The gloss "(Thanh tra UBCKNN)", which the text does not state, was removed, along with "cấp tỉnh". The basis now names NĐ 128 for cl. 2 Art 47(d). The key is unchanged.
  - **L4-018**: the explanation asserted the current status of the securities inspectorate → now limited to what Art 7, 61, 62.2 and 63 say.
- **L4-031 (1/2 rule).** In the Vietnamese NĐ 306 text, only cl. 2 Art 5 is amended (item 3 of Art 1). Cl. 3(c) Art 5 (NĐ 128) is untouched: Chapter II fines apply to organisations except Art 15.3–5, Art 30.2 and Art 39.3/5. Art 32 is not among them, and individuals pay 1/2.
