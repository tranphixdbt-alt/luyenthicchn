                                CIRCULAR
                Providing regulations on securities practicing

      Pursuant to the Law on Securities No. 54/2019/QH14, as amended and
supplemented by Law No. 56/2024/QH15;
      Pursuant to the Government’s Decree No. 155/2020/ND-CP dated
December 31, 2020, detailing the implementation of certain articles of the Law
on Securities, as amended and supplemented by Decree No. 245/2025/ND-CP;
      Pursuant to the Government’s Decree No. 29/2025/ND-CP dated February
24, 2025, defining the functions, tasks, powers, and organizational structure of
the Ministry of Finance, as amended and supplemented by Decree No.
166/2025/ND-CP;
      At the proposal of the Chairman of the State Securities Commission;
      The Minister of Finance hereby promulgates this Circular providing
regulations on securities practicing.


                                    Chapter I
                           GENERAL PROVISIONS
      Article 1. Scope of regulation and subjects of application
      1. Scope of regulation:
      This Circular provides regulations on:
      a) Securities professional certificates and equivalent certificates;
      b) Electronic securities practicing certificates;
      c) Professional knowledge training for securities practitioners.
      2. Subjects of application:
       a) Individuals participating in training courses for securities professional
certificates;
       b) Securities practitioners and organizations employing securities
practitioners;
      c) Other relevant organizations and individuals.
      Article 2. Interpretation of terms
       1. Securities practice certificate is a document (in paper or electronic form)
issued by the State Securities Commission (SSC) to the person named in the
certificate to work at securities companies, securities investment fund
management companies, branches of foreign securities companies in Vietnam,
branches of foreign fund management companies in Vietnam, and securities
investment companies operating legally within the territory of the Socialist
Republic of Vietnam.
       2. Securities professional certificate is a document (in paper or electronic
form) issued by the Securities Research and Training Center under the SSC to
certify that the person named in the certificate has met the requirements in
securities and securities market professional proficiency examinations.
      3. Organizations employing securities practitioners include securities
companies, securities investment fund management companies, branches of
foreign securities companies in Vietnam, branches of foreign fund management
companies in Vietnam, and securities investment companies.


                                    Chapter II
 SECURITIES PROFESSIONAL CERTIFICATES AND EQUIVALENT
                     CERTIFICATES
Article 3. Securities professional certificates
      1. Training courses for securities professional certificates include:
      a) Course on basic issues of securities and the securities market;
      b) Course on Law on securities and the securities market;
      c) Course on Securities analysis and investment;
      d) Course on Securities brokerage and investment consultancy;
      đ) Course on Financial consultancy and securities underwriting;
      e) Course on Corporate financial statement analysis;
      g) Course on Fund and asset management;
      h) Course on Derivatives and the derivatives market.
      2. The courses prescribed in Clause 1 of this Article are organized by the
Securities Research and Training Center under the SSC or by training facilities

under universities in collaboration with the Securities Research and Training
Center.
        3. Securities professional certificates shall be issued by the Securities
Research and Training Center within fifteen (15) days to examniees who satisfy
the requirements in a professional proficiency examination (including persons
who completed the training courses held by training facilities under universities
in collaboration with the Securities Research and Training Center).
       4. The SSC shall approve the training programs for the courses described
in Clause 1 of this Article.
        5. Cases exempted from attending professional training courses but
required to take professional proficiency examinations in order to be issued
securities professional certificates include:
        a) Individuals holding a bachelor's degree or higher in the following
majors: Economics, Business, Finance - Banking - Insurance, Accounting -
Auditing are exempted from the course on basic issues of securities and the
securities market;
      b) Individuals holding a bachelor's degree or higher in Economic Law are
exempted from the course on Law on securities and the securities market.
      Article 4. Certificates equivalent to securities professional certificates
       1. A Certified International Investment Analyst (CIIA) certificate or a
written confirmation of passing the Chartered Financial Analyst (CFA) level II
exam or higher is equivalent to certificates in: Basic issues of securities and the
securities market, Securities analysis and investment, Corporate financial
statement analysis, Fund and asset management, Derivatives and the derivatives
market.
       2. A Association of Charterted Certified Accountants (ACCA) certificate,
a Certified Public Accountants (CPA) certificate or the auditor certificate,
accountant certificate issued by the Ministry of Finance, or a written confirmation
of passing a Chartered Financial Analysis (CFA) level I or a Certified
International Investment Analysis (CIIA) level I exam, are equivalent to
certificates in: Basic issues of securities and the securities market; and Corporate
financial statement analysis.
      3. A legal foreign securities practice certificate or equivalent documents
proving that an individual is permitted to practice securities legally abroad is
equivalent to all securities professional certificates.

                                    Chapter III
      ELECTRONIC SECURITIES PRACTICING CERTIFICATES
       Article 5. Conversion of paper-based securities practicing certificates
into electronic securities practice certificates
       1. The SSC shall commence the issuance of electronic securities practicing
certificates upon the official operation of its information technology system for
the management of securities practitioners. From this point onward, the SSC shall
cease the issuance of paper-based securities practicing certificates.
       2. Upon the SSC’s implementation of electronic securities practicing
certificate issuance, securities practicing certificate holders and organizations
employing such practitioners shall be responsible for converting previously issued
paper-based securities practicing certificates into electronic ones within 01 (one)
year from the date the SSC issues the conversion guidelines.
       A paper-based securities practicing certificate shall become invalid upon
its conversion. The certificate holder is responsible for surrendering the
invalidated paper-based securities practicing certificate to the SSC.
       3. Beyond the time limit specified in Clause 2 of this Article, any securities
practicing certificate holder who has not completed the conversion shall not be
recorded as having the status of a "securities practitioner" on the SSC’s
information technology system for the management of securities practitioners.
       4. The electronic practicing certificates and the history of securities practice
recorded on the SSC’s information technology system for the management of
securities practitioners shall serve as the basis for the SSC to manage and
supervise certificate holders in accordance with Article 216 of Decree No.
155/2020/ND-CP dated December 31, 2020.
       A securities practicing certificate holder shall only be recognized as
practicing securities when the information technology system for the management
of securities practitioners confirms their status as a "securities practitioner".
     Article 6. Responsibilities of securities practicing certificate holders
and organizations employing securities practitioners during the
implementation of electronic securities practicing certificates
       1. Securities practicing certificate holders and organizations employing
securities practitioners shall be responsible for complying with the regulations and
guidelines issued by the SSC regarding the management and use of electronic
securities practicing certificates.
      2. Securities practicing certificate holders shall be responsible for updating
any changes related to their securities practicing history on the SSC’s information
technology system for the management of securities practitioners.


                                          4
       3. Organizations employing securities practitioners shall be responsible for
updating the SSC’s information technology system for the management of
securities practitioners with any changes occurring upon the execution or
termination of labor contracts with securities practicing certificate holders.
      4. The entities specified in Clause 2 and Clause 3 of this Article shall be
responsible for ensuring the accuracy, completeness, and truthfulness of the
information provided on the SSC’s information technology system for the
management of securities practitioners.
      Article 7. Use of electronic securities practicing certificates and look-
up of securities practicing status
       1. Securities practitioners may directly present their electronic securities
practicing certificates via the SSC’s information technology system for the
management of securities practitioners.
       2. Individuals and organizations may look up the securities practicing status
of a securities practitioner on the SSC’s information technology system for the
management of securities practitioners.


                                   Chapter IV
               PROFESSIONAL KNOWLEDGE TRAINING
                 FOR SECURITIES PRACTITIONERS
      Article 8. Professional knowledge training
      1. Securities practitioners must participate in annual professional
knowledge training (the period is calculated from January 1 to December 31 of
each year).
       2. Securities practitioners are not required to participate in professional
knowledge training during the year in which their securities practicing certificate
is issued.
      Article 9. Contents and materials of professional knowledge training
       1. Training contents: Laws on securities and the securities market, trading
systems, and new types of securities. Based on the training contents, the SSC shall
issue a professional knowledge training framework before December 31 of each
year. The framework may be adjusted or supplemented to suit practical situations.
      2. Training materials:
      a) Professional knowledge training materials shall be developed by the
organizing units in the form of hard copies or electronic data;
       b) Professional knowledge training materials must be consistent with the
training framework specified in Clause 1 of this Article.

                                         5
      Article 10. Organization of professional knowledge training
       1. Organizing units: The Securities Research and Training Center under the
SSC shall preside over and coordinate with the Vietnam Stock Exchange and its
subsidiaries, and the Vietnam Securities Depository and Clearing Corporation and
its subsidiaries.
      2. Securities practitioners shall register for professional knowledge training
classes in person or online.
      3. The duration of professional knowledge training classes shall be
designed to suit the training contents, materials, and participants, ensuring a
minimum training duration of 08 hours per year.
     4. The SSC shall issue the Regulations on the organization of professional
knowledge training for securities practitioners.
      Article 11. Cases of failure to fulfill professional knowledge training
requirements
       Before January 31 of each year, the SSC shall publicly disclose the list of
securities practitioners who failed to fulfill the professional knowledge training
requirements of the previous year on its portal and the information technology
system for the management of securities practitioners.


                                    Chapter V
               EFFECTIVENESS AND IMPLEMENTATION
      Article 12. Effectivenesss and implementation
      1. This Circular takes effect on February 09, 2026. Circular No.
197/2015/TT-BTC dated December 03, 2015 of the Minister of Finance providing
regulations on securities practice is hereby repealed.
       2. The SSC shall issue the Regulations on the management and use of the
SSC’s information technology system for the management of securities
practitioners in accordance with the provisions of this Circular regarding the
management and use of electronic practicing certificates and the look-up of
securities practicing status.
       3. The organization of professional knowledge training for securities
practitioners as prescribed in Chapter IV of this Circular shall be implemented
from January 1, 2027.
      4. In case the legal normative documents cited in this Circular are amended,
supplemented, or replaced by other legal normative documents, the provisions of
the amending, supplementing, or replacing documents shall be applied.
