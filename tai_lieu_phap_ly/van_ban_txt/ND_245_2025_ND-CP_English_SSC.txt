                                    DECREE
Amending and supplementing some articles of the Government's Decree
 No. 155/2020/ND-CP dated December 31, 2020 on elaboration of some
                  articles of the Law on Securities


      Pursuant to the Law on Government Organization No. 63/2025/QH15;
      Pursuant to the Law on Enterprises No. 59/2020/QH14 amended and
supplemented by the Law No. 03/2022/QH15 and the Law No. 76/2025/QH15;
      Pursuant to the Law on Investment No. 61/2020/QH14 amended and
supplemented by the Law No. 03/2022/QH15;
      Pursuant to the Law on Securities No. 54/2019/QH14 amended and
supplemented by the Law No. 56/2024/QH15;
      At the request of the Minister of Finance;
       The Government promulgates the Decree amending and some articles of
the Government's Decree No. 155/2020/ND-CP dated December 31, 2020
detailing the implementation of some articles of the Law on Securities.
     Article 1. Amending and supplementing some articles of the
Government's Decree No. 155/2020/ND-CP dated December 31, 2020
detailing the implementation of some articles of the Law on Securities as
follows:
     1. Amending and supplementing some points and clauses of Article
3 as follows:
     a) Adding Point h after Point g, Clause 12, Article 3 as follows:
    "h) The ending date of issuance of shares for conversion of convertible
bonds is the date determined by the issuer for the conversion of convertible
bonds into shares.".
     b) Amending Point c, Clause 14, Article 3, adding Point d after Point
c, Clause 14, Article 3 as follows:
     "c) In case of enterprise acquisition or assets sale, this ratio shall be
calculated as the transaction value to the total asset value of the enterprise based
                                         2

on the latest annual financial statements. In case of making many transactions
to acquire one or many enterprises in which the acquired enterprises belong to
the group of parent companies - subsidiaries, this ratio is calculated as the total
transaction value arising in the restructuring year to the total asset value of the
enterprise;
     d) The indicator "total asset value" specified at Points a, b and c of this
Clause shall be based on the latest audited annual financial statements; in case
the enterprise has an affiliated unit that does not have legal status, it shall be
based on the general financial statements; in case the enterprise is a parent
company, the total asset value shall be based on the lower value between the
parent company's separate financial statements and the consolidated financial
statements.".
     c) Adding Clause 57 after Clause 56 of Article 3 as follows:
     "57. Independent credit rating organizations include:
     a) Credit rating agencies Moody's, Standard & Poor, Fitch Ratings;

     "1. Responsibilities of organizations and individuals related to documents

      a) Organizations and individuals participating in the process of preparing,
certifying, signing documents and reports related to activities of offering,
issuance, listing, registration of trading, registration, depository, clearing and

     b) Consulting organizations and practitioners participating in document

     Article 3. Transitional provisions
     1. Organizations and individuals that have been licensed or approved by
SSC, the Stock Exchange or VSDC before the effective date of this Decree are
not required to carry out procedures for licensing and re-approval under the
provisions of this Decree.
      2. Organizations and individuals that have submitted valid documents and
reports to SSC and the Stock Exchange before the effective date of this Decree
shall continue to comply with the provisions of Decree No. 155/2020/ND-CP.
     3. Public companies that have not yet carried out the procedures for
notifying the maximum foreign ownership ratio as prescribed in Decree No.
155/2020/ND-CP are responsible for completing the notification of the
maximum foreign ownership ratio within 12 months from the effective date of
this Decree.
     4. Public companies that have completed notifying their maximum foreign
ownership ratio or a change in that ratio as prescribed at Point e, Clause 1,
Article 139, Point c, Clause 4, Article 141, Point d, Clause 1, Article 142 of
Decree No. 155/2020/ND-CP shall follow:
      a) Continue to comply with the notified maximum foreign ownership ratio
until they announce a change in that ratio as prescribed at Point b of this Clause;
                                         50

      b) Public companies may change the maximum foreign ownership ratio
under the GMS Resolution, provided that the new ratio is higher than the
previously notified ratio and does not exceed the limits specified in Points a, b,
c, d, dd, Clause 1, Article 139 of Decree No. 155/2020/ND-CP. The dossier for
this change must include the documents stipulated in Clause 2, Article 142 of
Decree No. 155/2020/ND-CP, as amended and supplemented by Points c and
d, Clause 58, Article 1 of this Decree, along with the GMS Resolution; the order
and procedure for notifying this change are to be carried out in accordance with
Clause 3, Article 142 of Decree No. 155/2020/ND-CP, as amended and
supplemented by Point dd, Clause 58, Article 1 of this Decree.
     5. Public companies as prescribed at Point d, Clause 1, Article 11 of Law
No. 56/2024/QH15 may offer shares to existing shareholders according to the
shares ownership ratio through public offering of securities in accordance with
the provisions of this Decree and Decree No. 155/2020/ND-CP until their
public company status are revoked.
     6. In case an individual has obtained the results of the examination before
the effective date of this Decree and submits an application for a securities
practitioner certificate after the effective date of this Decree, the certificate will
be issued in accordance with the legal provisions that were in force at the time
of the examination.
     For candidates taking or retaking the failed part of the securities
practitioner certificate examination where the application period began before
the effective date of this Decree, the examination and certificate issuance will
be conducted according to the legal provisions that were in force on the first
day of that application.
      For candidates retaking the failed part of the securities practitioner
certificate examination where the application period began after the effective
date of this Decree, application for the examination and issuance of securities
practitioner certificates shall be submitted as prescribed at Clause 4, Article 213
of Decree No. 155/2020/ND-CP, the examination and certificate issuance shall
be carried out according to the provisions of this Decree.
      7. When SSC implements the issuance of electronic practitioner
certificates, the previously issued paper practitioner certificates shall be
converted into electronic versions under the guidance of SSC. The paper
version becomes invalid from the time of conversion.
