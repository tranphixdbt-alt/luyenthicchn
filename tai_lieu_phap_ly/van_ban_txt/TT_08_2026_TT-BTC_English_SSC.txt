Article 4. Amendments and additions to certain clauses of Article 6
1. Amendments and additions paragraph 1 as follows:
“1. Investors must comply with the following regulations:
a) Investors open securities trading accounts at securities companies that are trading members to
execute securities transactions; for non-resident foreign investors, placing securities trading orders
is done through a foreign securities business organization representing them using the foreign
investor's custody account number or directly by the foreign investor using their own trading
account;
b) From the date the clearing and settlement of securities transactions under the central
counterparty clearing mechanism is implemented, investors must have a clearing margin account
at a clearing member before conducting securities transactions. The opening of a clearing margin
account by investors shall comply with the law on clearing and settlement of securities
transactions.
2. Amendments and additions paragraph 3 as follows:
3. Investors are only allowed to open one clearing and settlement margin account at the clearing
member corresponding to each securities trading account, except in certain cases as stipulated by
law on clearing and settlement of securities transactions.
3. Amendments and additions Section 7 as follows:
7. Foreign securities companies and foreign securities investment fund management companies
are allowed to open two securities trading accounts at each securities company as follows:
a) One securities trading account to conduct the company's own trading activities;
b) One securities trading account to manage the company's clients' transactions.
Article 5. Amendments and additions to certain clauses of Article 7
1. Amendments and additions point b, clause 2 as follows:
“b) Securities companies may only enter orders into the securities trading system when investors
have met the margin requirements for clearing as stipulated by law on clearing and settlement of
securities transactions.”
2. Amendments and additions paragraph 5 as follows:
This English translation is for reference only. If there are any discrepancies between the English
and Vietnamese versions, the Vietnamese version shall prevail

“5. Securities companies are responsible for controlling order placement at their companies for
buy-sell transactions within the same periodic order matching session when receiving and entering
customer orders into the stock exchange's securities trading system.”
3. Amendments and additions paragraph 6 as follows:
6. Foreign securities companies and foreign securities investment fund management companies
are permitted to use trading accounts as stipulated in point b, clause 7, Article 6 of this Circular to
place both buy and sell orders for the same security in each matching session (periodic or
continuous) or negotiated transaction, but must ensure that the buy and sell orders for securities
are not from the same foreign investor.
Article 6. Clause 1a is added after Clause 1 of Article 9a, which was supplemented by Clause
2 of Article 1 of Circular No. 68/2024/TT-BTC.
“1a. In cases where a foreign investor, which is an organization, fails to pay for the shares
purchased by the securities company as stipulated in Clauses 6, 7, and 8 of Article 40k of Circular
No. 119/2020/TT-BTC , the securities companies receiving the investor's trading orders must
ensure that the investor's share purchase orders are executed as follows:
a) Foreign institutional investors are not permitted to place buy orders for shares that do not require
sufficient funds to place an order within 7 consecutive trading days from the day immediately
following the day on which the foreign institutional investor fails to fulfill its payment obligations
as stipulated;
b) Within 30 consecutive trading days, if a foreign institutional investor commits a third violation
of payment obligations for a share purchase transaction that does not require sufficient funds at
the time of placing an order, the foreign institutional investor shall be prohibited from placing a
share purchase order that does not require sufficient funds at the time of placing an order for a
period of 180 consecutive trading days from the trading day immediately following the day on
which the foreign institutional investor commits the third violation of payment obligations;
c) The Vietnam Stock Exchange shall notify its member securities companies that foreign
institutional investors must have sufficient funds in their custody accounts when placing buy orders
for shares during the period when such investors are prohibited from trading shares that do not
require sufficient funds as stipulated in points a and b of this clause, after receiving written
notification from the Vietnam Securities Depository and Clearing Corporation that the foreign
institutional investor has failed to fulfill its transaction payment obligations as stipulated.”

organization authorized to represent non-resident foreign investors, using the foreign investor's
custody account number to place securities trading orders for the foreign investor.

5. Responsible for signing contracts with clients or foreign securities business representatives
when providing services to clients; providing complete and truthful information to clients and
foreign securities business representatives.”
Article 9. Amendments and additions to certain clauses of Article 13
1. Amendments and additions paragraph 3 as follows:
3. Data on brokerage accounts of clients opening accounts at securities companies, and data on
transactions of foreign investors using custody account numbers to trade securities through foreign
securities business organizations represented at securities companies must be centrally managed
and kept in backup at another location.
2. Amendments and additions point d, clause 4 as follows:
“d) Receiving transaction orders from a person other than the account holder without written
authorization from the account holder, except in cases where a foreign investor uses a custody
account number to trade securities through a foreign securities business organization as a
representative; settling transactions with a person other than the account holder without written
authorization from the account holder .”
Article 10. Amendments and additions to certain provisions of Article 14
1. Amendments and additions paragraph 2 as follows:
2. Securities companies are obligated to update changes in client information when requested by
the client or a foreign securities business representative.
2. Amendments and additions paragraph 3 as follows:
3. Securities companies must sign trading account opening contracts with customers, except as
stipulated in Article 15a of this Circular . Securities companies must directly execute securities
transactions for customers and are legally responsible for these activities.
3. Amendments and additions paragraph 4 as follows:
4. Securities companies must keep detailed records of each client's funds and securities, providing
information on balances and transactions of funds and securities to clients upon request, except in
cases where clients open custody accounts with a custodian member that is not a trading member.
Article 11. Amendments and additions to Clause 1 of Article 15
“1. To conduct securities trading transactions for clients, securities companies must open trading
accounts for each client based on a securities trading account opening contract with the client,
except as stipulated in Article 15a of this Circular . Clients are responsible for providing complete
and accurate client identification information when opening a securities trading account. The
account opening contract must comply with current regulations and contain at least the minimum
contents as prescribed in Appendix III issued with this Circular.”
Article 12. Add Article 15a after Article 15.
Article 15a. Use of custody account number for securities transactions
1. Foreign securities business organizations may use the custody account number of foreign
investors to place securities trading orders on behalf of non-resident foreign investors at the
securities company.
This English translation is for reference only. If there are any discrepancies between the English
and Vietnamese versions, the Vietnamese version shall prevail

2. Securities companies must identify foreign investors and foreign securities business
organizations acting as representatives in accordance with the law on anti-money laundering.
3. Foreign securities business organizations may use the custody account number of foreign
investors to conduct securities trading transactions for foreign investors based on a contract
between the foreign securities business organization and the securities company.
4. Foreign securities business organizations acting as representatives are responsible for providing
information on foreign investors and information on securities transactions of foreign investors
when requested by securities companies or state agencies.
5. The contract between a securities company and a foreign securities business organization acting
as its representative must include at least the following contents:
a) Agreement on receiving and placing orders for securities transactions;
b) Agreement on the price of securities trading services and other costs incurred when executing
the transaction;
c) Agreement on verifying foreign investors, verifying foreign securities business organizations
acting as representatives, and confirming trading orders before entering orders into the trading
system;
d) Providing information on foreign investors and information on securities transactions of foreign
investors when requested by securities companies or state agencies;
d) Confirmation of the investor's payment obligation to the securities company when executing a
stock purchase transaction does not require sufficient funds to be available at the time of placing
the order;
e) Agreement on the payment obligations of foreign institutional investors in cases where the
investor does not have sufficient funds and other incurred costs (if any);
g) Agreement on the obligations, rights, and responsibilities of the parties to the contract and any
related parties (if any).
6. When receiving securities trading orders from foreign investors through a foreign securities
business representative, securities companies and foreign securities business representatives must
adhere to the following principles:
a) Securities companies must take measures to verify and ensure that orders are received from the
representative foreign securities business organization that is a party to the contract as stipulated
in Clause 5 of this Article; ensure that all information is fully recorded at the time of order receipt,
retain evidence proving the order placement, and confirm the order placed by the representative
foreign securities business organization for foreign investors for reference when necessary;
b) The foreign securities business organization acting as a representative shall take measures to
ensure that all information and evidence regarding the order placement of foreign investors are
fully recorded for reference when necessary.”

organizations acting as representatives in one of the following forms:

2. Amendments and additions paragraph 4 as follows:
4. Securities companies may only execute trading orders when the order contains complete and
accurate information about the client, method, type of order, trading date, time of receipt, securities

company with the time (day, hour, minute) of receipt at the time of order receipt.

order, the securities code, the quantity, and the transaction price. The securities company must
record the time (date, hour, minute) of receipt of the customer's transaction order at the time of
receipt.
3. Amendments and additions Section 7 as follows:
7. Securities companies must notify the results of order execution to the client or the representative
of the foreign securities business organization immediately after the order is matched, using the
method agreed upon in the contract between the client or the representative of the foreign securities
business organization and the securities company.
